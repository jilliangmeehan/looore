We're a little over a month away from the release of 5.8, which means we're coming up on the end of our year in Natlan, and we're on the cusp of whatever comes next. If you haven't been playing Genshin much lately, this might be an exciting (but overwhelming!) time to get back into it. 

**First of all:** Don't stress. I may be really into Genshin's lore, but you do not have to be to enjoy the story. They will explain everything that needs to be explained, and they will remind you about things you might have forgotten. Chances are, if you're confused about something, you are supposed to be confused about it.

**Second of all:** Don't rush. We're still a few months away from Nod-Krai, and there is no requirement to catch up with everything as fast as possible. If you have quests to finish or areas to explore, it is *always* better to take your time and enjoy things at your own pace — rushing through stuff just to finish it is not a recipe for a good time, and it's not actually going to set you up well for future updates or make you excited to play again.

Now, if you're ready to get ready for Nod-Krai, I've compiled everything that I think is worth checking out right here. You don't need to read or watch all of it — it's just here so you don't have to go digging for it yourself if you're interested.

## The story so far
If you'd like a recap of what happened in Natlan before we move on, I recommend [Ashikai's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eOP3F9lBeHQ). It's just over an hour long, and it covers the entire Natlan Archon Quest. 

> [!jillian] 
> I definitely recommend catching up with the Archon Quest and finishing the "[Little One](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Between_Pledge_and_Forgettance
> )" world quest series. The latter probably isn't required to advance the main story, but it provides a lot of context about the ancient dragon race in Teyvat that I think will be relevant in Nod-Krai.

## Absolute bare minimum TL;DR
- The next nation we are going to, in 6.0, is [Nod-Krai](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Nod-Krai)
	- Nod-Krai is an "autonomous region" 
	- It is still basically part of Snezhnaya
- The theme of Nod-Krai is the [[Moon|moon]]
	- This part of the story is called "Song of the Welkin Moon" 
	- Nod-Krai is going to feature some special kind of moon-related power, rather than Cryo
- We're going to see people from a lot of different [[Song of the Welkin Moon|factions]] in Nod-Krai — some old friends, some people we know but have never met, and some new people entirely
	- Highlights: Varka, Alice, Dottore
- There will be a Nod-Krai Prologue quest in 5.8

## Diving deeper
### Event hints
As usual, the last time Liben showed up in Mondstadt, he started giving us hints about our next destination. You can read the full dialogue [here](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Marvelous_Merchandise/2024-11-11/Story). He introduced Nod-Krai to us as a place "in the southernmost part of Snezhnaya, where people from all over Teyvat gather," and mentioned the Voynich Guild.

In one of [Jean's birthday letters](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Mail/Mother..._(2025-03-14)), we heard that her mother (a Knight of Favonius) was ordered to return to Dornman Port in Mondstadt with a few other knights, while Varka and the rest of his expedition headed to Nod-Krai. And much more recently, in one of Thoma's Travelers' Tales [anecdotes](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Thoma:_Long_Vacation), we found out that *his* mother (also a Knight of Favonius) was just called out to Nod-Krai to join Varka's expedition.

In the "[On the Trail of Behemoths](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/On_the_Trail_of_Behemoths/Story)" event, we met a kid named Helka who had a lot to say about Nod-Krai. Helka also left us with some notes, which you can read [here](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Helka%27s_Notes).

> **Helka:** Well, about that... Nod-Krai is actually part of Snezhnaya, and once upon a time, they performed lots of large scale development in the area.
> 
> **Helka:** But you can't find hide nor hair of that nowadays — unless you're talking about the mechanical scrap you can see everywhere.
> 
> **Helka:** But maybe that's precisely why Nod-Krai's become the bustling place it is today.
> 
> ...
> 
> **Helka:** If I had to describe it, I'd go with "a dangerous yet lively place"? It's been leaning toward the former in recent days, though.
> 
> **Helka:** Before I left, the Fatui had moved a lot of construction materials in. Word was that a Harbinger — or Harbingers — were present, too. As for what they intend, I couldn't say.
> 
> **Helka:** I've heard bad things about the situation to the north as well. The Abyss's influence has been expanding... Katya even warned me not to return via the northern paths.

Helka also mentioned the Frostmoon Scions, and says they live in the wilds on an island and worship a moon deity, with statues they've made all over Nod-Krai (I have a feeling these will be our Statues of the Seven alternative). The island the Scions live on supposedly also has a lot of ancient relics that resemble the Secret Source Automatons from Natlan — which, as we know, are left over from the dragons.

> I've seen relics of a similar style in Nod-Krai. Perhaps the little island we live on is like a big ship, and was once linked to Natlan, only to be dragged off northward by some gargantuan deep sea creature with its fins, claws, or tentacles... or something, which might explain why the area in which we can find similar things is so tiny.
> 
> ‒ [Helka's notes](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Helka%27s_Notes)
### Artifact sets
The two newest artifact sets, [[Long Night's Oath]] and [[Finale of the Deep Galleries]], both have some Nod-Krai lore in their descriptions. Those two pages have the full description text, plus some of my messy notes about them. 

> [!jillian] 
> Keep in mind that while artifact sets released around the time of a new nation usually give us some interesting lore to chew on, it's also usually impossible to know what the stories are actually about. [Nymph's Dream](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Nymph%27s_Dream) ended up being about something very different from what I imagined, and the Natlan artifact sets gave us a shape of the nation's history but obscured a lot of important context that we learned later on.
> 
> That said, we can at least start to piece some things together — like the different places and factions we might encounter in Nod-Krai and what their history might look like.
