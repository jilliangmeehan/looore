The [Four Shades](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Shades) were created by and from the Primordial One, and they seem to have had a lot of influence over almost every corner of Teyvat, but we don't really hear much about them.

The last Shade is still a mystery to us, but we can at least guess that they are either the Shade of Void or the Shade of Reason — I'm leaning toward the former, with the Primordial One representing Reason.
